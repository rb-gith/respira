    About Respira

    Slow breathing with a long, gentle exhale helps the body shift into its calm, resting mode. Five minutes a day is a good start.

    • Cyclic sighing: in a 2023 Stanford study, 5 minutes a day improved mood and slowed breathing more than mindfulness meditation.
    • 4-7-8: the long exhale is what helps the heart rate settle.
    • Resonance: about 5.5 breaths a minute, a pace linked to higher heart rate variability.
    • Box: four equal steps, used to stay steady under stress.
    • Belly: slow breathing into the belly, studied for lowering stress.

    Stop if you feel dizzy or light-headed, and breathe normally. Skip the breath holds if you are pregnant or have a heart or lung condition. Respira is not a substitute for medical advice.
